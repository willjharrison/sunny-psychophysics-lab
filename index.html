Sunny Psychophysics

We study the mind (psycho) using physics (physics) at the Sunshine Coast (sunny).

Welcome to the Sunny Psychophysics Lab at UniSC. We investigate the computational mechanisms of visual perception, visual search, and spatial vision. In other words, we try to figure out how photons hitting your retina end up convincing your brain that you saw something that may or may not actually be there.

Our research combines psychophysics, Gabor filter modeling, Bayesian inference, and the occasional attempt to quantify why visual scene ambiguity makes us both uncomfortable and entirely unable to look away.
